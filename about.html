🏥 Our Story

A Legacy Built on Trust & Compassion

Founded in 2001 with a modest 50-bed facility and a singular vision, Nirmala Medical Center has grown into the region's most trusted multi-specialty hospital. Over two and a half decades, we have served more than one lakh patients, pioneered life-saving technologies, and earned the trust of families across generations. Our journey is defined not by the walls we have built, but by the lives we have transformed.

Today, our 500-bed state-of-the-art campus houses 50+ specialist doctors, cutting-edge robotic surgery suites, a 24/7 trauma center, and digital health platforms that bring world-class care to every patient's fingertips. NABH accredited and ISO 9001:2015 certified, we set the benchmark for clinical excellence in the region.

📅 Our Journey

25 Years of Milestones

From a humble 50-bed clinic to a 500-bed multi-specialty hospital — trace the milestones that shaped Nirmala Medical Center.

🏥
2001
The Beginning
Nirmala Medical Center was founded by Dr. N. Krishnamurthy with a 50-bed facility and a team of 8 dedicated doctors, serving the local community with primary and secondary care.
🏗️
2005
Expansion to 200 Beds & ICU
Expanded to 200 beds with the inauguration of a modern Intensive Care Unit (ICU), Trauma Center, and multi-specialty OPD. Doctor count grew to 25 specialists.
🏆
2008
NABH Accreditation
Became one of the first hospitals in the region to achieve NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals) accreditation, affirming our commitment to national quality standards.
❤️
2012
Cardiac Center of Excellence
Launched a dedicated Cardiac Center of Excellence with catheterization labs, open-heart surgery facilities, and a specialized cardiac rehabilitation program.
🏨
2015
500-Bed Multi-Specialty Hospital
Completed the major expansion to 500 beds with 12 clinical departments, modern operation theatres, NICU, and a state-of-the-art radiology suite featuring 3T MRI and 128-slice CT.
🤖
2018
Robotic Surgery Unit Inaugurated
Inaugurated the region's first robotic surgery unit, offering minimally invasive procedures with sub-millimetre precision across urology, gynecology, and general surgery.
💻
2020
Digital Health Transformation
Launched online appointment booking, telemedicine consultations, digital patient portal, and AI-assisted triage — bringing world-class care to patients' homes during and beyond the pandemic.
✅
2023
ISO 9001:2015 Certification
Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification across all departments, reinforcing our quality management systems and commitment to continual improvement in patient safety and service delivery.
🎉
2026
Celebrating 25 Years of Excellence
Marking a silver jubilee of healthcare excellence with the launch of a new Cancer Care Center, expanded telemedicine network, and commitment to serve the next generation with even greater innovation and compassion.
