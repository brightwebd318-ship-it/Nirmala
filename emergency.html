🚨 What We Treat

Emergency Services We Offer

Our emergency department is equipped to handle all life-threatening conditions with speed, precision, and compassion.

🦺

Advanced Trauma Care

Full-service Level I trauma center equipped for road accidents, fall injuries, penetrating trauma, blast injuries, and polytrauma. Rapid triage and immediate surgical intervention available.

⚡ Action: Immediate trauma assessment, CT scan, emergency surgery within 15 minutes of arrival
❤️‍🔥

Cardiac Emergency

24/7 cath lab for heart attacks, acute coronary syndrome, life-threatening arrhythmias, and cardiac arrest. Door-to-balloon time under 60 minutes for STEMI cases.

⚡ Action: ECG in 5 min, troponin tests, primary PCI angioplasty, defibrillation & pacing
🧠

Neurological Emergency (Stroke)

Dedicated stroke unit with a clot-busting team available around the clock. CT angiography, tPA thrombolysis, and mechanical thrombectomy for ischemic stroke within the golden hour.

⚡ Action: Stroke code activation, CT brain scan in 10 min, tPA within 60 min of arrival
👶

Pediatric Emergency

Dedicated pediatric emergency bay with child-friendly staff, neonatal care, febrile seizures, respiratory distress, anaphylaxis, and accidental poisoning management.

⚡ Action: Pediatric triage, IV access, seizure control, respiratory support, NICU escalation
🌸

Obstetric Emergency

Emergency obstetric care for eclampsia, postpartum hemorrhage, placenta previa, ectopic pregnancy, and preterm labor. Neonatal ICU (NICU) support immediately available.

⚡ Action: Obstetric triage, fetal monitoring, emergency C-section within 30 min, NICU standby
☠️

Toxicology & Poisoning

Management of drug overdose, pesticide poisoning, snake bite, food poisoning, and chemical exposure. Gastric lavage, antidote therapy, and dialysis available on-site.

⚡ Action: Toxicology screen, gastric lavage, antidote administration, activated charcoal, dialysis if required
